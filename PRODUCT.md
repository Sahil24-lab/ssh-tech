# SSH Tech

## Register

Brand website.

## Platform

Responsive web, built in the existing Next.js application.

## Purpose

SSH Tech is Sahil Harriram's engineering and technical-systems practice. The site positions the business around engineering software and intelligent systems for complex physical operations.

Dedicated AI consulting, workflow transformation, and production AI delivery belong under Modais. SSH Tech may use AI where it improves a broader engineering system, but AI is not the sole product or sales proposition.

## Primary audience

Engineering and operations leaders in industrial, infrastructure, robotics, connected-product, construction, and Defence-adjacent organisations.

## Primary outcome

Start a qualified project conversation through the existing contact and booking flow.

## Positioning

Engineering software and intelligent systems for complex physical operations.

SSH Tech works across software, embedded systems, controls, connected hardware, and applied intelligence. It is a founder-led specialist practice with the range to work across technical boundaries and the commercial experience to carry a project into delivery.

## Page architecture

1. Hero with a calm animated engineering scene and a project CTA.
2. Capabilities across engineering software, connected systems, robotics and controls, and AI-enabled engineering tools.
3. A system flow that explains where applied intelligence fits.
4. Selected work using resume-backed examples and outcomes.
5. A three-stage engagement model without public pricing.
6. Existing verified testimonials, reproduced in a new SSH Tech component.
7. A concise founder section.
8. Contact CTA with booking and email paths.

## Voice

Direct, technical, grounded, and commercially aware. Use plain English. Prefer concrete systems and outcomes over abstract claims. Write as SSH Tech using "we", while making the Sahil-led model clear.

## Proof boundaries

- Use only supplied resume facts and existing verified testimonials.
- Do not invent client metrics, project volumes, sector coverage, or technical capabilities.
- Avoid exact employment dates because the resume versions conflict.
- Do not imply that every project includes AI.

## Visual direction

Use the existing Signal Workshop system in `DESIGN.md`: Deep Channel, Field Green, Signal Teal, Near White, and Supporting Cyan. The page should feel like a precise technical workshop, not a SaaS template or science-fiction interface.

The hero uses the supplied connected raster artwork with a satellite, robotic arm, rocket trajectory, and meaningful signal nodes. Motion is slow and subtle, with a static reduced-motion treatment.

## Non-goals

- Do not edit, import, or replace the existing `AI...` landing components.
- Do not remove or alter the current `ai.ssh-tech.xyz` content.
- Do not publish pricing tiers.
- Do not revive the Web3 positioning on the apex site.
- Do not use neural-network imagery, humanoid robots, cyberpunk styling, or decorative technical grids.

## Accessibility

Target WCAG 2.2 AA contrast, keyboard-visible focus, semantic landmarks and headings, 44px touch targets, responsive layouts, and `prefers-reduced-motion` support.
